Key rule: outline first. Cut, merge, or rename leaves here before anyone builds a block. Every block takes its tokens and contracts from the fundamentals DropFast (FILLED.md and tokens.json). No OvenLovin skin yet. Category names and counts are for planning only; no Tailwind Plus code is copied.

How to review

  • Cut a leaf or a whole category you will not need.
  • Merge leaves that differ only by a token (dark, accent, surface) into one block with a variant.
  • Rename anything unclear. Reply with the changes, then building starts in slices.
